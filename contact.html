Frequently Asked Questions

How early should we start planning our launch campaign?

We recommend starting 3 to 6 weeks before your target opening date. This allows creators to build curiosity and anticipation before opening day, coordinate first-look tastings or walkthroughs, and ensure opening week footfall is maximized.

What size businesses do you work with?

We work with independent restaurants, artisan bakeries, boutique fitness studios, salons, fashion outlets, emerging consumer brands, and multi-location regional concepts. Any business where local word-of-mouth matters.

Do you handle creator coordination and content approvals?

Yes. We manage the end-to-end process: creator discovery, outreach, briefing, scheduling visits, content deliverables, and reporting so you can focus entirely on running your business.
