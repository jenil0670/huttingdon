Spec Campaign

Drafted

Reimagining how India designs its next home.

The Approach

From an idea to a floor plan.

To introduce Drafted to India's design-conscious audience, Huttingdon would turn everyday home ideas into creator-led transformations.

Interior and lifestyle creators would start with familiar prompts — ""What would you build here?"", ""Design my dream home"", or ""Can AI turn this idea into a real floor plan?"" — and use Drafted to transform them into visual concepts.

The campaign would make the product instantly demonstrable: creator idea → AI-generated design → final reveal.

By combining interior design, architecture, real estate and aspirational lifestyle creators, the campaign would reach people actively thinking about their next home, renovation or dream space.

The Results

Turning inspiration into action.

The campaign is designed to make Drafted a product people can immediately understand and imagine using.

By showcasing real ideas transforming into usable designs, the creator content would drive audiences from inspiration → curiosity → sign-up, while building awareness around Drafted as an AI-powered tool for bringing home ideas to life.
