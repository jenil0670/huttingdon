Our Purpose

Local businesses deserve
local influence.

Modern marketing has made it incredibly easy to reach people. It has not necessarily made it easier to matter to them.

The Problem We Solve

Physical businesses compete online,
but customers live in real places.

A restaurant may offer an unforgettable dining experience, yet remain invisible to people five kilometres away. A new lifestyle outlet can open without generating meaningful anticipation. A local business can spend money on advertising without building genuine trust.

At the same time, regional creators already have the attention and trust of these communities. Their followers turn to them daily for recommendations: "Where should we go? What should we try?"

"Huttingdon exists to connect those two worlds."

Businesses that need attention. Creators who already have it. Communities that decide where to spend their time and money.

OUR MISSION

More Relevant Creator Marketing

To make creator marketing more relevant to the businesses and communities around us. Where businesses can discover relevant creators, creators discover meaningful opportunities, and audiences discover businesses through people they already trust.

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

The Regional Creator Ecosystem

An infrastructure connecting Business × Creator × Community. We understand local creator ecosystems deeply enough to know which creators influence which neighbourhoods, categories, and moments.

Our Philosophy

Principles that guide our work.

We don't believe in vanity numbers, fake results, or meaningless marketing claims.

01 // PHILOSOPHY

Local > Generic

A local audience can matter exponentially more than a massive generic audience distributed across continents.

02 // ALIGNMENT

Relevance > Reach

Reach is useful only when the people reached have the intent and ability to visit your business.

03 // PLANNING

Strategy > Followers

A list of influencers is not a strategy. We start with the business moment, timeline, and desired real-world outcome.

04 // INTEGRITY

Trust > Promotion

Creator collaborations succeed when content feels natural to the creator and genuinely valuable to their followers.

05 // IMPACT

Attention > Noise

The objective is never to create brief fleeting noise. It is to generate meaningful, lasting community attention.

06 // RELATIONSHIPS

Long-Term > One-Off

We build enduring bridges between businesses, creators, and neighbourhoods rather than isolated transactions.
