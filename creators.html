Got Questions?

Creator Network FAQ

Is there any fee or commission to join?

No. Joining the network is completely free. We work directly with commissioning businesses to fund campaigns.

Does joining the network mean giving up my management?

Absolutely not. You retain full independence. Joining simply means our strategy team considers your profile when an opportunity in your city matches your content style.

Do you only work with creators who have massive follower counts?

No. We care about who is listening and where they live. A creator with 15,000 followers in a specific neighbourhood can often create more footfall for a restaurant than an account with 500,000 disconnected followers.
