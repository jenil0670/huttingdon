Spec Campaign

Airbuds

Making music taste social in India.

The Approach

Making music taste social.

We would introduce Airbuds to India's Gen Z audience through creators who turn everyday music habits into moments of discovery. From friend reactions and music-taste reveals to campus challenges and ""music twin"" content, the campaign would make Airbuds feel less like another app — and more like a new way to see what your people are listening to.

The Results

From attention to connection.

The campaign is built to move audiences from creator discovery to installation and friend invites, creating a repeatable acquisition loop around the social nature of music.
