Spec Campaign

Wispr Flow

Speak it. Flow with it.

The Approach

What if you stopped typing?

Huttingdon would introduce Wispr Flow to India's productivity-focused audience through one simple demonstration: say what you want to write.

Productivity, coding, startup and career creators would showcase real workflows — writing emails, drafting documents, coding, taking notes and responding to messages — while switching from traditional typing to voice.

The creative would focus on the speed difference, making the product's value visible rather than explaining it.

Think → Speak → Done.

The campaign would combine productivity-focused creators with technology and developer creators to position Wispr Flow as an everyday AI productivity layer for people who spend their day behind a keyboard.

The Results

From typing less to doing more.

The campaign is designed to make Wispr Flow immediately useful in the viewer's mind.

By demonstrating familiar tasks being completed through voice, creator content would move audiences from ""that's interesting"" → ""I need this"" → product adoption, establishing Wispr Flow as a faster way to communicate, work and create.
